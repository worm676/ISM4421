    How the signals work

    • 200 WMA is the average of the last 200 weekly closes (about 4 years). It shows the long-term trend.
    • DOWN PRESSURE: price is more than 30% below the 200 WMA.
    • WEAK BUY: price is 10–30% below the 200 WMA.
    • STRONG BUY: price is at the 200 WMA or up to 10% below it.
    • BUY ZONE: price is 0–10% above the 200 WMA.
    • WATCH: price is 10–30% above. EXTENDED: price is more than 30% above.
    • Crypto Sweetspot runs the same process on major cryptocurrencies (those with 200+ weeks of history): coins within 6% of their own 200 WMA. For crypto, Bitcoin's position versus its 200 WMA stands in for the S&P 500 as the market check.
    • Sweetspot lists stocks within 6% of the 200 WMA, either above or below it, where a bounce off long-term support is most likely. Good buy setups are listed first, then stocks marked ↑ Turning up (trading above last week's close).
    • Market phase: when the S&P 500 is above its own 200 WMA the market is in a long-term bull phase, and pullbacks in quality stocks are more attractive. When it is below, be more selective and defensive.
    • Setups combine the signal with the market and the stock's own trend. Good buy setup price is within 10% below to 5% above a rising 200 WMA while the market is in a bull phase. Be selective the same zone, but the market is in a bear phase. Breakdown risk the stock's 200 WMA is falling, so it's a breakdown in a downtrend, not a pullback. Wait for pullback 5–10% above the WMA: don't chase.
    • Enter with discipline: the entry plan splits a position into thirds at 5% above, at, and 5% below the 200 WMA, with a stop under the last third sized to the stock's typical weekly move. Shares are sized so hitting the stop loses only your chosen risk per trade.
    • Patience: this is a long-term, low-frequency strategy, so expect only a few good setups a year. The 200 WMA is a timing tool, not a substitute for studying the business.
    • RVOL (relative volume) is the last completed period's volume divided by its average. A reading of 1.5× or higher alongside a buy signal counts as volume confirmation.
